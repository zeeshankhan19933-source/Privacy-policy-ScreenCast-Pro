1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how ScreenCast Pro ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our Android application. This policy has been created in strict accordance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the User Data Policy, Permissions Policy, and Family Policy.

By downloading, installing, or using ScreenCast Pro, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To give you full peace of mind when capturing and streaming your screen:

  • No Personal Identity: We do not require registration, login, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords to use the App.
  • No Media Uploads: Screen recordings and screenshots are generated locally on your Android device and saved directly to your device storage (MediaStore). We operate no remote cloud storage servers that receive your video or screenshot files.
  • No Keystroke or Screen Logging: We do not inspect, log, or record confidential text, credit card inputs, passwords, or personal messages. Capture only begins upon your deliberate initiation.

3. Android Permissions and How They Are Used

ScreenCast Pro strictly adheres to the principle of least privilege. Each permission requested serves a specific, user-facing core function:

Permission API / Type Purpose & Implementation Details
MediaProjection System Capture API Captures display frames to produce screen video recordings and screenshots. Capture starts exclusively after you explicitly accept the Android system dialog. A permanent indicator and stop control is shown in your status bar during capture.
RECORD_AUDIO Microphone / Internal Audio Records audio commentary or internal gameplay/app sounds during recording sessions. Audio is encoded into the MP4 media stream locally on your device and is never transmitted to us.
CAMERA Camera2 API Powers the optional Facecam front-camera preview window while gaming or making tutorial videos. Camera video is rendered locally on screen and is not stored or shared anywhere without your knowledge.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Display over other apps Enables the floating overlay widget so you can start, pause, stop recording, or snap a screenshot while inside any game or full-screen app without needing to switch back to ScreenCast Pro.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notifications (Android 13+) Displays the active recording or streaming foreground service notification, allowing you to monitor elapsed time and stop recording with one tap.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Foreground Service Types (Android 14+) Complies with Android 14+ requirements using dedicated service types: mediaProjection, camera, and microphone to maintain capture reliability when the App is in the background.

4. Live Streaming & RTMP Credentials

ScreenCast Pro allows users to broadcast live directly to RTMP destinations (such as YouTube Live, Facebook Live, or custom RTMP servers):

  • Direct Connection: Live stream video and audio packets are encoded on your device and transmitted directly over RTMP/RTMPS sockets to your target destination server. Streams do not pass through intermediate proprietary servers.
  • Encrypted Stream Keys: Stream keys and server URLs you enter are stored locally on your device in secure app storage. We never transmit or share your stream keys with any third party.

6. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Your Media Stays Yours: All screen recording MP4 files and screenshot PNG/JPEG files are stored on your device in the standard Android public media directories (Movies/ScreenCastPro and Pictures/ScreenCastPro).

  • You retain 100% control over retention. You can rename, share, or delete any recording at any time from within the App's "Library" tab or through your preferred Gallery/File Manager application.
  • Deleting a file in the App deletes it permanently from local device storage.
  • Uninstalling the App does not automatically erase your saved video captures if they were saved to the system MediaStore.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

ScreenCast Pro does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The App is intended for general audiences interested in screen recording, gaming commentary, and tutorial creation. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so that we can take appropriate measures.

8. Security Standards

We implement industry-standard security practices to safeguard your settings and stream profiles:

  • Sensitive configuration data is isolated within Android's protected application sandbox (EncryptedSharedPreferences and private internal storage).
  • Live RTMP and RTMPS connections utilize standard cryptographic protocols supported by the destination platform.
